# Privacy Policy — Atom QR

**Last updated:** October 21, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Atom QR is a browser extension that helps users generate QR codes from website URLs or text, download QR code images, and access locally saved QR code history.

## 2. Information We Process

When you use Atom QR, the extension processes the URL or text you enter to generate a QR code. If you choose to generate a QR code for the current tab, the extension accesses that tab's URL for that action.

## 3. Third-Party QR Generation Service

Atom QR uses the external service provided by [QRServer](https://api.qrserver.com/) to generate QR code images. The URL or text submitted for QR generation is sent to this service. Its processing of that information is subject to the service provider's own privacy practices and terms.

Please avoid submitting confidential or sensitive information through the extension.

## 4. Local Storage

Atom QR may store QR code history and extension preferences locally in the browser using extension storage. This information is used to provide the extension's features.

## 5. Information Sharing

Atom QR does not operate its own account system or backend server for collecting user information. However, information submitted for QR generation is transmitted to the third-party QR generation service described above.

## 6. Data Security

Users should avoid entering sensitive or confidential information into Atom QR. Data sent to the third-party service is subject to that service's policies and practices.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Atom QR is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the extension's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

## 9. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the extension publisher through the support contact information provided in the Microsoft Edge Add-ons listing, if available.
